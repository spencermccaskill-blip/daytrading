How a setup earns a signal

  • Rules and the pass bar are written down before the test is run. No changes after.
  • Average at least +0.10R after a 1 point cost, t-stat at least 2.
  • Positive in each of 2024, 2025 and 2026, and over the last 12 months.
  • Worst drawdown no deeper than 10R, and under $2,000 on MNQ at $250 risk.
  • Passed setups signal. Everything else is a stand-down. A weak setup is never shown to fill the space.
