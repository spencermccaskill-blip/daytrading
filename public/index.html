How the suggestions have done

Finished suggestions from the replay log, at the rule exits, before commissions. This covers weeks, not years: a setup with a handful of trades can look great or awful by luck alone. The count is beside every number for that reason.

The two win rates

  • Last 4 weeks is the big number on the Desk. It comes from the replay log: what each setup's suggestions actually did recently. It is usually only a few trades.
  • Since 2024 is the long test on about three years of history, a few hundred trades per setup. It moves slowly and is the better guide to whether a setup has an edge.
  • A setup can win often and still lose money if its losses are bigger than its wins, so the average result per trade (in R) is shown next to each win rate.

What "Not proven" means

  • Rules and the pass bar were written down before each test was run.
  • To pass: average at least +0.10R after a 1 point cost, t-stat at least 2, positive in 2024, 2025, 2026 and the last 12 months, drawdown no deeper than 10R and under $2,000 on MNQ at $250 risk.
  • Full write-up: research/RESULTS.md and research/PREREGISTRATION.md in the app folder.
