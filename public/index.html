The rules

  1. Wait for the sweep. After 9:30 ET, price trades through the Asian, London (2 to 5 ET) or prior day high or low.
  2. Enter on the 15m close. A 15-minute bar closes through the last 3 bars, against the sweep, between 9:45 and 11:00 ET.
  3. Stop past the extreme. One point beyond the session high or low since 9:30 ET.
  4. Take 2R. Target is twice the risk. No moving it.
  5. Flat by noon. Out at 12:00 ET whatever happens. One trade a day.
  6. Skip what doesn't fit. If one contract risks more than your budget, there is no trade.
