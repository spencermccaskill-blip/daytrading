File: `day48_pt2.txt`. Length: about 15.5 minutes (last marker 0:15:21), 3,065 words. A re-recording: the original (about 30 minutes, recorded before the news) had no audio, and he refers to it several times.

Summary: "Live testing" on a PPI day. He sets a daily bias for each of four instruments, marks where price should draw to, and builds a mock trade without trading. It contains the most complete statement of the entry sequence in this set, the equilibrium confirmation rule, the stop location, and the rule that the daily (not weekly) trend sets the bias.

Bias rules:

- A daily break against the weekly is a weekly retrace toward weekly building blocks. [0:06:41] "the daily break of structure is causing a weekly retrace into one of these building block areas."
- Alignment needed. [0:07:42] "ideally, we have high time frame aligned, high time frame aligned, high time frame aligned, small ... time frame primed for execution."
- "Primed for execution" defined. [0:07:42]-[0:08:13] "the 1-hour is in an uptrend, the 4-hour is in an uptrend, the daily is in an uptrend"; "the 15-minute just broke structure to the downside because it's drawing towards like an hourly ... building block".

- What restores a tradable state. [0:10:48]-[0:11:18] "If we get a break of structure back to the upside on the four hour and then a break of structure to the upside on the one hour"; [0:11:18] "then we're waiting for execution points."

## Programmable rules from days 40-48

Each rule is limited to what he states. Where a parameter is missing from his words, it is marked "not stated" and is not filled in.

1. News-day filter: no trades on a day with CPI, PPI or core PPI, or unemployment claims. (Day 43 [0:01:02]; Day 47 [0:14:54]-[0:15:25] "we avoid it all together"; Day 48 [0:01:01].)
2. Not a no-trade trigger: preliminary consumer sentiment (Day 43 [0:02:03]); an FOMC member speaking (Day 48 [0:01:01]).
3. GBP news: do not trade GBP pairs until the scheduled GBP release is out. (Day 43 [0:00:31].)
4. Do not enter on a break of structure that forms at the news release. (Day 47 [0:11:35]-[0:12:05].)
5. Session filter: trade New York or London session only; not Asian session. (Day 46 [0:03:04].)
6. Bias timeframe: when weekly and daily trend disagree, the daily trend is the bias. (Day 48 [0:09:15].)
7. Direction filter: never take a setup opposite to the daily bias. (Day 44 [0:09:00]; Day 47 [0:03:38]; Day 48 [0:09:47].)
8. Alignment filter: require daily, 4-hour and 1-hour structure to point the same way as the bias; otherwise skip the instrument. (Day 44 [0:12:36]-[0:13:06]; Day 45 [0:13:51]; Day 47 [0:11:04]-[0:11:35]; Day 48 [0:10:48].)
9. Re-arm condition after a conflict: wait for a 4-hour break of structure and then a 1-hour break of structure in the bias direction. (Day 48 [0:10:48]-[0:11:18]; 1-hour only, for GJ: Day 48 [0:07:11], [0:08:43].)
10. Weekly outlook condition: look for trades in the weekly/daily direction only while 1-hour and 4-hour agree. (Day 43 [0:03:38].)
11. Hierarchy classification: a break of structure on a timeframe against the trend of the next timeframe up (5-minute vs 15-minute, 15-minute vs 1-hour, 1-hour vs 4-hour, daily vs weekly) is treated as a retrace, not a bias change. He says "not every single time". (Day 44 [0:12:05]; Day 47 [0:04:40]-[0:05:40]; Day 48 [0:07:42].)
12. Bias change: a higher-timeframe break of structure against the bias changes the bias. (Day 41 [0:09:39]; Day 43 [0:07:47]-[0:08:18].) A higher-timeframe bearish bias holds until price closes above the relevant swing high. (Day 47 [0:10:34].)
13. Setup state ("primed for execution"): daily, 4-hour and 1-hour in the bias direction, and the 15-minute has broken structure against it toward an hourly building block. (Day 48 [0:07:42]-[0:08:13].)
14. Entry sequence: (a) liquidity sweep, (b) break of structure in the bias direction, (c) retest into an order block, fair value gap or equilibrium, (d) reaction. Sweep plus break alone is not an entry. (Day 41 [0:03:03]; Day 46 [0:09:39]; Day 48 [0:03:34]-[0:04:05].) See contradiction 1.
15. Equilibrium confirmation: if the retest is into equilibrium only, require a candle to close back beyond the equilibrium line in the trade direction; a single candle in the trade direction is enough at an order block or fair value gap. (Day 48 [0:04:36]-[0:05:07].)
16. Stop: beyond the extreme of the liquidity sweep (below it for a long). (Day 48 [0:05:07].)
17. Targets: multiple partial targets (three in live trades, two in the mock) placed at prior draws: liquidity highs/lows, untouched order blocks, 4-hour candle bodies, candle highs, and on Forex/gold the Asian session high. (Day 41 [0:07:06]; Day 46 [0:00:00]; Day 47 [0:08:30]-[0:09:02], [0:12:05]; Day 48 [0:05:07].) Which level is chosen is not rule-defined.
18. Risk per trade: 1% to 3% of account, ideally 1%; above 3% is a rule breach. (Day 46 [0:05:34].)
19. No building block reached equals no trade, even with full alignment. (Day 47 [0:14:24]; Day 48 [0:13:21].)
20. Forex/gold session levels: record Asian session high and low and London session high and low; setup is a London high or low taken during New York session, followed by a break of structure the other way. (Day 47 [0:07:25]-[0:08:30], [0:11:35], [0:13:22]-[0:13:53].)
21. Pre-market: liquidity levels (15-minute and hourly lows/highs) and bias are fixed before the open; bias review 30 minutes before the New York open. (Day 44 [0:01:08]; Day 48 [0:03:03]-[0:03:34].)
