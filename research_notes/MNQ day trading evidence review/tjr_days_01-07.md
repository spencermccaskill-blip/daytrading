## Programmable rules from days 1-7

Each rule below is stated by him in words precise enough to code. Where a parameter is missing it is listed under "Needs judgement", not filled in.

1. **Candle parts.** For any timeframe, one candle covers that timeframe's duration. Up candle: close above open. Down candle: close below open. Top of upper wick = highest price in the period; bottom of lower wick = lowest. (Day 2, 0:03:11, 0:04:41, 0:05:13, 0:06:16, 0:06:46)
2. **Closed candles only.** Make decisions only from candles that have closed, never from the candle still forming. (Day 2, 0:16:43, 0:17:13; Day 6, 0:21:14)
3. **High.** A high forms when an up candle is followed by a down candle. Its price is the higher of the two candles' wick tops. (Day 6, 0:02:04, 0:04:09 to 0:04:40, 0:19:11)
4. **Low.** A low forms when a down candle is followed by an up candle. Its price is the lower of the two candles' wick bottoms. (Day 6, 0:02:04 to 0:02:36, 0:04:40)
5. **A forming candle is not a high.** The current candle's wick is not a high until the next candle is a down candle; then the high moves there. (Day 6, 0:21:14)
6. **Uptrend.** Each new high is higher than the previous high and each new low is higher than the previous low. (Day 4, 0:02:04 to 0:02:35, 0:21:03)
7. **Downtrend.** Each new high is lower than the previous high and each new low is lower than the previous low. (Day 4, 0:03:06, 0:21:03)
8. **Which swing to watch.** In an uptrend watch only the most recent low. In a downtrend watch only the most recent high. Older highs and lows are dropped, even if an older low is lower. (Day 6, 0:08:47, 0:14:32 to 0:15:02, 0:18:10 to 0:18:40)
9. **Break of structure to the downside.** In an uptrend, a candle closes below the most recent low. (Day 6, 0:16:38, 0:29:00)
10. **Break of structure to the upside.** In a downtrend, a candle closes above the most recent high. (Day 6, 0:07:47, 0:21:14)
11. **Wick does not count.** A wick beyond the most recent high or low without a close beyond it is not a break of structure. He labels these "liquidity sweeps" or fake-outs. (Day 6, 0:12:24, 0:12:57, 0:23:18 to 0:23:48, 0:25:51)
12. **After a break of structure,** treat the trend as flipped to the direction of the break. (Day 6, 0:06:44, 0:09:17; Day 4, 0:04:38)
13. **Timeframe ranking.** Monthly outranks weekly, weekly outranks daily; in general higher timeframes outrank lower. (Day 6, 0:22:16; Day 4, 0:08:13)
14. **Counter-trend break is a pullback.** A daily break of structure against the weekly trend is treated as a retracement, not a new trend. (Day 6, 0:22:47 to 0:23:18)
15. **Direction filter.** Take lower-timeframe trades only in the direction of the higher-timeframe trend. If the lower timeframe points the other way, wait until it turns to agree. (Day 4, 0:05:39, 0:08:44, 0:15:54)
16. **Higher timeframe for intraday.** For intraday trades, the bias comes from the four hour or the daily "at least". (Day 4, 0:16:56 to 0:17:28)
17. **Timeframe stack example.** With four hour and daily bearish: a liquidity sweep on the 30 minute, then a break of structure to the downside on the 5 minute. Given as an example of a good short, not as a complete entry rule. (Day 4, 0:10:15 to 0:10:45)
18. **No trade from a candle shape alone.** A doji or wick is one "confluence" and never a trade by itself. (Day 2, 0:11:33, 0:13:07, 0:14:09)
19. **No trade from a new high alone.** Price pushing above a high in an uptrend is not an entry signal. (Day 4, 0:05:08 to 0:05:39)
20. **Instrument limit.** Work on one or two instruments only. (Day 4, 0:27:13)

Process rules that can be turned into a checklist: journal every day (Day 1, 0:40:37); write a weekly, multi-day and daily direction forecast before trading (Day 4, 0:23:39 to 0:25:42); journal each trade and review what was right and wrong (Day 5, 0:07:13, given as an example goal).
