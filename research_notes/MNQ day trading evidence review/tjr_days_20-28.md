## Programmable rules from days 20-28

Each rule is only what he states. Where he states it two ways, both are listed as variants and no choice is made here.

1. **Bullish order block, location.** After a break of structure to the upside, the order block is the down leg immediately before the up move that broke structure. (Day 22 0:04:06, 0:22:16; day 24 0:07:07)
2. **Bearish order block, location.** After a break of structure to the downside, the order block is the up leg immediately before the down move that broke structure. (Day 20 0:02:45; day 22 0:01:33, 0:22:48; day 24 0:12:16)
3. **Sweep condition.** Variant A: the leg must have taken out a prior low (bullish) or prior high (bearish). (Day 20 0:03:46, 0:07:49.) Variant B: the sweep is preferred, not required. (Day 24 0:09:41)
4. **Zone boundaries.** Variant A (his advice for learners): the full price range of the leg, high to low. (Day 24 0:10:13, 0:25:10.) Variant B (his own): for a bullish order block, from the wick down to the start of the body. (Day 24 0:11:46.) Which candle is meant in B is not stated.
5. **One per trend.** Only one order block exists per trend; a new one is created only at the next trend shift (next break of structure in the opposite direction). (Day 20 0:07:49 to 0:08:49)
6. **Order block appears only after the break.** The zone is marked once structure has broken, then price must retrace into it. (Day 24 0:08:08; day 22 0:24:53)
7. **Order block entry, variant A (confirmation).** After price enters the zone, go to a lower timeframe and enter when a candle closes through the swing high being monitored (long) or swing low (short). He does not say which swing point; see "Needs judgement". (Day 22 0:12:53, 0:16:29, 0:20:42; day 24 0:17:22)
8. **Order block entry, variant B (touch).** Enter as soon as price touches the order block. (Day 26 0:09:23, 0:14:27)
9. **Timeframe ladder used in his examples.** Daily or 4-hour break of structure and order block, then 1-hour break of structure, then 15-minute break of structure for entry. Nested version: after the 1-hour break, mark the 1-hour order block, wait for price to fill it, then take the 15-minute break. (Day 22 0:11:21 to 0:13:25, 0:20:08 to 0:20:42; day 24 0:20:32 to 0:21:33)
10. **Session filter.** Do not take the lower-timeframe entry before the session opens (New York or London); start monitoring highs or lows for a break only after the open. (Day 22 0:12:21 to 0:12:53, 0:15:59.) The clock time is not given.

16. **Range anchors and update.** Uptrend: from the leg's low to the newest high; each time a new high is made before price reaches 0.5, move the top anchor to the new high. Downtrend: from the high to the newest low; move the bottom anchor to each new low. (Day 28 0:05:44, 0:06:14, 0:08:20, 0:18:18)
